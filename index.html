Got Questions?

Everything attendees ask us before they book.

Is there a hackathon at DevConf 2026?

Yes - Track 8 runs a 24-hour build sprint on the final day, open to every ticket holder, with prizes for the top three teams.

Will sessions be recorded?

Every talk is recorded and added to your dashboard within 48 hours, so a clashing schedule never means missing out.

What's the dress code?

Come as you are. Most attendees wear whatever they'd code in on a regular Tuesday.

Can I get a refund if my plans change?

Passes are transferable to a colleague up to 7 days before the event, and fully refundable up to 30 days out.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes - step-free entry, accessible restrooms on every floor, and live captioning for all keynote sessions.
